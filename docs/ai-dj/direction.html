Three ways to play

Your role can change with the night.

AI Only

AI runs the performance and smoothly corrects ordinary manual changes. Disarm remains available at all times.

B2B

You take priority. AI respects replacement songs, paused decks and pinned controls. It can retake released controls at a later eligible transition.

Playlist Only

AI organizes the set. You load, cue and mix. No AI performance commands. Session observations and history remain available.

The engineering limits we will test

MIDI carries control and metadata, not audio. Node timers do not promise exact execution times. The first milestone measures host feedback, UI, audio and takeover under load. Missing playlist, cue or capture operations get narrow host extensions. All 22 feature families stay in the inventory; unknown coverage stays visible.

Every feature needs separate command, feedback, native UI and runtime evidence. The HTML simulation proves none of those runtime capabilities. Other DJ hosts and Raspberry Pi deployment are outside this build.

Why this direction

Build the intelligence around
an instrument we can inspect.

Mixxx gives us visible decks and a working audio engine. Its source lets us investigate gaps in control, state and library access. Our effort goes into musical decisions, dependable execution and a collaboration you can understand.

Automatic choices stay editable. Missing songs are explained. Human changes become part of the music and the record. An AI recommendation is always distinct from what actually happened.
