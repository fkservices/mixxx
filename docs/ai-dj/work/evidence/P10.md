Move operations fill only eligible remaining slots. Active/protected, fixed, loaded, pre-listened, playing, terminal and reconciliation-required occurrences stay fixed. Interrupted occurrences may be edited only when not actively protected. These guards do not replace current host-state validation; callers must supply an authoritative protection snapshot, and P11/later admission work must validate the wider musical invariants before execution.

User removal becomes an explicit operator skip with request identity and logical next-playable-or-end intent. The occurrence remains in the admitted set and full planned order, and its source row and partial span history remain intact. AI cannot issue operator skips or pin changes. Adding a song creates a fresh occurrence and pending outcome, advances set/history versions, and preserves the source snapshot. Library identity must match. Playlist-only admission outside the supplied pool requires a specific human override; that exception is audited and does not switch the music-pool policy. AI additions outside the pool require free-library policy.

A new pin of the same kind explicitly replaces the prior one, with the request and prior version retained in the audit/archive history.

Duplicate notices are rebuilt by library-scoped track identity and retain every occurrence. Existing sections, original source and observed spans are preserved.
