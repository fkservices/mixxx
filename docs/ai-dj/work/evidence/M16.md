All four workers had advanced at the first telemetry sample and continued advancing between every subsequent sample. The workload averaged 3.74 CPU cores; raw one-second CPU/memory/batch telemetry and a host/benchmark/load process snapshot are retained. The workload started before benchmark launch and stopped after its terminal result. No music files or MIDI were accessed by the workload. This is a defined synthetic workload, not a production AI-model or full-machine saturation benchmark.
