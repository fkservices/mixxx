## Design validation

Walked these cases against the documented boundaries:

- An unmapped CC and repeated unchanged CC are each copied before normal mapping decisions; neither is suppressed from raw recording.
- F8/FE and long SysEx are observed before host-side filtering/truncation; backend filtering failures and unsupported endpoints keep coverage incomplete.
- Interleaved real-time data, short-message padding and interrupted SysEx retain backend provenance. No converter invents bytes or a completed gesture.
- A read error reports unknown backend loss, while ring saturation reports exact producer gaps. A final watermark makes trailing loss visible without requiring a later MIDI packet.
- Controller reopen, mapping reload, service loss and capture disable retire epochs and partial attribution. No reconnect reuses authority or resumes capture without the configured policy.
- Consumer/recorder overload cannot block or mutate normal dispatch. Packet storage, endpoint count, batch size, outstanding batches and retry lifetime have explicit limits.
- Actor labels and host effects need separate evidence. B2B recognition cannot rely solely on bulk recording delivery; priority manual-intent/dispatch integration remains required.
